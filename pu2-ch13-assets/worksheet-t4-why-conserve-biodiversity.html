Section A — Multiple Choice Questions (1 mark each · tick the correct option)
1. The use of about 25,000 plant species in traditional medicine is an example of which argument for conserving biodiversity?
(a) Broadly utilitarian argument
(b) Ethical argument
(c) Narrow utilitarian argument
(d) Aesthetic argument
2. The Amazon forest is estimated to produce approximately what percentage of the total oxygen in the Earth’s atmosphere?
(a) 20%
(b) 50%
(c) 6%
(d) 35%
3. Which of the following is NOT counted among the pollinators that provide the ecosystem service of pollination?
(a) Bumblebees
(b) Bats
(c) Butterflies
(d) Earthworms
4. “Every species has an intrinsic value, even if it has no current economic value to us.” This statement belongs to which argument?
(a) Narrow utilitarian
(b) Ethical
(c) Broadly utilitarian
(d) Economic
5. Match the benefit with its correct category: (P) Tannins and resins, (Q) Flood and soil erosion control, (R) Aesthetic pleasure.
(a) P – broadly utilitarian; Q – ethical; R – narrow utilitarian
(b) P – ethical; Q – narrow utilitarian; R – broadly utilitarian
(c) P – narrow utilitarian; Q – broadly utilitarian; R – broadly utilitarian
(d) P – narrow utilitarian; Q – narrow utilitarian; R – ethical
Section B — Fill in the Blanks (1 mark each)
1. The narrow utilitarian argument is based on the benefits that humans derive directly from nature, such as food and fibre.
2. About plant species are used in traditional medicines across the world.
3. The Amazon forest is estimated to produce about of the total oxygen in the Earth's atmosphere.
4. The argument states that every species has an intrinsic value, even if it has no economic value to us.
5. Pollination, flood control and aesthetic pleasure are examples of arguments for conserving biodiversity.
Section C — 2 Mark Questions
1. What is meant by the narrow utilitarian argument for conserving biodiversity? Give any two examples.
2. What is the ethical argument for conserving biodiversity?
3. Mention any two ecosystem services provided by biodiversity.
