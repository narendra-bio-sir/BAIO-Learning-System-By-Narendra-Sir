STEP 5

Frequently Asked Questions

My code says "invalid" — what do I do?

Double-check three things: (1) you're logged in as yourself, not a classmate's account, (2) you've typed the code exactly, including the dash, and (3) you're using the right code type — a Handwritten Notes code won't open Premium Content, and vice versa. If it still doesn't work, contact your teacher — see Get Help below.

Can I use a friend's code if I lose mine?

No — every code is locked to one student's name, roll number, and section, so a friend's code will never unlock content for you. Ask your teacher to resend your own code instead.

Do codes expire?

Once a chapter is unlocked on your account, it stays unlocked — you don't need to re-enter the code again unless you sign into a new device or your account is reset.

I changed my name or roll number — will my old codes still work?

No, because codes are tied to your exact details at the time they were made. Let your teacher know about the change and they'll issue you fresh codes.

What's the difference between Premium Content and Handwritten Notes?

Premium Content is the full digital chapter — videos, explanations, and solved practice questions. Handwritten Notes are your teacher's own written revision notes for that chapter, best used for quick recap before a test.
