Why Biology Leads

  • 1Biology posts the highest average score of any subject in both the Slip Tests (58.9% combined) and the Monthly Test (70.8%).
  • 2Biology has the lowest failure rate among core subjects in both assessments — 15.05% combined in Slip Tests, and just 5 failures (out of 80 who appeared) in the Monthly Test.
  • 3Biology shows the highest topper density — 27.96% scoring >15/20 in Slip Tests, and 25 students (the most of any subject) scoring 35+/40 in the Monthly Test.
  • 4Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics consistently show failure rates above 25–55% across both assessment types — the gap with Biology is structural, not a one-test effect.
