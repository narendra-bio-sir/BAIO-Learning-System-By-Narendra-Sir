    ❓ Questions lecturers ask

    Is BAIO free for my students?

    Yes. Rapid Fire, Projects & Activities with worksheet PDFs, previous year questions, Diagram MCQs, flash cards, games, podcasts and the question paper tools are free. Some premium notes and books are in the BAIO Store. Some chapter pages open with an unlock code; message Narendra Sir to get codes for your class.

    Do students or lecturers need an account?

    No sign-up is needed. Open the link and start. Rapid Fire scores, activity records and game scores are saved only on the student's own phone.

    Can I use it on the classroom projector?

    Yes. BAIO runs in any browser on a laptop or smart board. Rapid Fire, Diagram MCQs, the activity simulators and the narrated Exam Corner slides work well on a big screen. Use the 🌙/☀️ button for a light theme in a bright room.

    Which languages are supported?

    Projects & Activities (with worksheet PDFs), flash cards and games are in English, Kannada and Hindi. The Exam Corner slides are narrated in all three languages. Science terms stay in English, as in the board exam.

    Will it work where the internet is weak?

    Yes. Install BAIO on the phone (install guide) and use Use BAIO Offline to save chapters. They then open without internet.

    Does BAIO follow the PU board syllabus?

    Yes. Content follows the NCERT textbooks used for the Karnataka PU board, chapter by chapter, with board blueprints, model papers and KCET/NEET previous year questions.
