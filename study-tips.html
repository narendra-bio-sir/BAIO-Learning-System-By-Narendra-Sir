01

How to Study Biology Theory

  • Active recall beats re-reading. After reading a topic, close the book and write or say out loud what you remember — then check what you missed. This is what actually builds memory, not passive reading.
  • Space your revision out. Revisit a topic at increasing gaps (next day, then 3 days later, then a week later) instead of cramming it once and moving on.
  • Redraw diagrams from memory. For diagram-heavy chapters (Plant Kingdom, Morphology, Anatomy), being able to recognize a diagram isn't enough — practice labelling it blank, from memory.
  • Write your own short notes in your own words after finishing a topic. The act of rephrasing is what forces real understanding, not just copying.
  • Use the app's own sequence: Study Mode/Concepts for your first pass, Flashcards to test recall the same day, then MCQ Quiz a few days later to check what actually stuck.
02

NEET Exam Strategy

  • Biology carries the most weight in NEET (90 of 200 questions) — it's the highest-yield subject per minute you put in, so consistency here compounds more than in any other subject.
  • Respect the negative marking. A wrong answer costs a full mark. Only attempt a question you can't fully solve if you've genuinely eliminated at least two options first.
  • Attempt in two passes. First pass: answer everything you're sure of. Second pass: come back for questions you can reason through. Don't burn time on a hard question during the first pass.
  • When two options both sound right, go back to the exact NCERT line — NEET Biology questions are frequently lifted almost word-for-word from NCERT, so the textbook's precise wording is the tiebreaker.
  • Diagram-based and assertion-reason questions repeat every year. Dedicated practice on these two formats pays off more than generic revision.
03

A Simple Weekly Rhythm

You don't need a complicated schedule — just repeat this loop for each new topic:

1
Same day the topic is taught — go through Study Mode / Concepts once, slowly.
2
That evening — run through Flashcards for that topic to test recall while it's fresh.
3
2–3 days later — attempt an MCQ Quiz on it. Wrong answers tell you exactly what to re-read.
4
Before each Monthly Test — use the Revision tab's Rapid Fire mode across every topic covered so far.
