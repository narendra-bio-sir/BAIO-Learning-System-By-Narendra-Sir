Step 3 · Where marks are actually won

The Practice Arsenal

Reading notes teaches you Biology. It does not teach you to answer 90 NEET questions in 60 minutes with negative marking, or to know which of two similar-looking KCET options is the trap. These five tools are the difference — and they're the only part of BAIO that's Premium.

⚠️
Biology is the single highest-weightage subject in NEET — 90 questions, 360 of 720 marks. It's exactly the subject where "I understood it while reading" and "I can answer it correctly under a clock, with wrong answers costing me marks" are two completely different skills. The tools below are what closes that gap. Skipping them isn't skipping a feature — it's walking into the exam having never actually rehearsed it.
Premium

MCQ Quiz

Topic-wise quiz drilling with instant scoring, feeding straight into your Progress log and Milestone badges — so you find out which topics are actually weak before the exam does.

Without it: you find out a topic was weak when you see the question in the exam, not before.
Premium

NEET Practice

Real NEET-pattern MCQs with a live per-question timer, full-screen exam mode, and genuine negative marking — from a bank deep enough to run 90-question sessions without repeats.

Without it: your first timed, negative-marked NEET Biology paper is the real one.
Premium

KCET Practice

Karnataka CET follows its own question pattern, distinct from NEET's — this bank is built to that pattern specifically, not just relabelled NEET questions.

Without it: preparing only in NEET's style is a common, avoidable reason strong students underperform specifically on the CET.
Premium

Paper Generator

Build a printable Theory or NEET-style test paper on demand — pick the topics, set the mark-wise question counts, get a paper you can sit for under real exam conditions.

Without it: your only "mock test" is the actual exam.
Premium

Board Important

The Karnataka PUC board blueprint for every chapter, laid out topic by topic — so your last week of revision goes to the topics that actually carry marks in the board paper, not spread evenly across all of them. Shown here in Biotechnology — every chapter gets its own.

Without it: a 1-mark topic and a 5-mark topic get the same revision time by default.
