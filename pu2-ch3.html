Board Important – Karnataka 2nd PUC Question Bank

A high-yield, almost entirely descriptive chapter in Reproductive Health – contraceptive methods, MTP, STDs, infertility and ART are asked as long answers nearly every year, and the terminology (IUD types, ZIFT/GIFT/IUT, oligospermia/azoospermia) is a favourite for 1–2 mark and MCQ questions in NEET/KCET too.
8 × 5 MOne from: contraceptive methods (barrier/IUD), MTP, STDs, or ART (GIFT/ICSI/AI) – almost always one full 5-mark answer from this chapter
9 × 3 MRCH aims, population explosion causes/steps, natural methods, oral pill composition, vasectomy vs tubectomy/MTP trends, STD complications, male infertility causes, ZIFT vs IUT
7 × 2 MAmniocentesis ban, condom advantages, emergency contraception, vasectomy vs tubectomy, STD curability, infertility definition, IVF popular name
1 M & MCQDefinitions/expansions – RCH, IUD, MTP, STD, PID, ART, IVF, ZIFT, GIFT, ICSI, LAM, oligospermia, azoospermia, cryptorchidism

⭐⭐⭐ Top priority – almost always asked (5 marks)

  • Barrier methods of contraception (5 M) – define the principle (physically preventing ovum–sperm union), then describe each device: condoms (thin rubber/latex sheath covering the penis in males or vagina/cervix in females; disposable, self-inserted, give privacy, and uniquely protect against STDs/AIDS – e.g. ‘Nirodh’); and diaphragms, cervical caps and vaults (reusable rubber devices covering the cervix, used with spermicidal creams/jellies/foams to boost efficiency).
  • Intra Uterine Devices – IUDs (5 M) – state the common action (increase phagocytosis of sperms in the uterus), then the three types with examples: non-medicated (Lippes loop – sterile inflammatory response, spermicidal tissue injury), copper-releasing (CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375 – Cu ions suppress sperm motility/fertilising capacity), hormone-releasing (Progestasert, LNG-20 – make uterus unsuitable for implantation, cervix hostile to sperm).
  • Medical Termination of Pregnancy – MTP (5 M) – cover definition, legal status (legalised in India in 1971 with strict conditions), global scale (45–50 million MTPs/year, ~1/5th of conceived pregnancies), safety by trimester (safe in first trimester <12 weeks; second trimester far riskier), and healthy vs unhealthy trends (safe medical abortion for rape/contraceptive-failure cases vs quack-performed illegal MTPs and misuse of amniocentesis for female foeticide).
  • ART – GIFT, ICSI, Artificial Insemination (5 M) – explain each in turn: GIFT (donor ovum transferred into the fallopian tube of a recipient who cannot produce her own ova); ICSI (a sperm directly injected into the cytoplasm of the ovum, used for very poor sperm count/quality); AI (semen from husband/donor introduced into the vagina or uterus, used for male-factor infertility or low sperm count).

⭐⭐ High priority – frequently asked (3 marks)

  • Aims of the RCH programme (3 M) – any five: creating awareness of reproductive organs/adolescence, sex education in schools, educating fertile couples about birth control, spreading knowledge of pre/post-natal care and breast feeding, awareness of population explosion and STDs, medical assistance for pregnancy/MTP/contraception/infertility, and equal opportunity for the male & female child.
  • Population explosion – causes & steps taken (3 M) – causes: rapid decline in death rate, falling MMR & IMR, more people in the reproducible age group; steps: statutory marriageable age (18 F/21 M), incentives for smaller families, the “HUM DO HAMARE DO” campaign, and wide promotion of contraceptives.
  • Natural methods of contraception (3 M) – periodic abstinence/rhythm method (avoid coitus on day 10–17, the fertile period), coitus interruptus (withdrawal before ejaculation), and lactational amenorrhea (no ovulation during intense lactation, effective up to 6 months post-parturition).
  • Composition, action & regimen of oral pills (3 M) – progesterone alone or progesterone–oestrogen combination; inhibits ovulation, inhibits implantation, alters cervical mucus; taken daily for 21 days starting within the first 5 days of the cycle, then a 7-day gap for menstruation.
  • Healthy vs unhealthy trends of MTP (3 M) – healthy: safe termination of unwanted/risky pregnancies, safest in the first trimester; unhealthy: illegal MTPs by unqualified quacks, and misuse of amniocentesis to detect and abort female foetuses.
  • Causes of infertility in males (3 M) – any three: impotency, endocrine disorders (e.g. hyposecretion of gonadotropins), non-functional testes/cryptorchidism, defective epididymis or vas deferens, oligospermia/azoospermia/asthenozoospermia.

⭐ Two-mark favourites – learn as clean pairs

  • Vasectomy (part of vas deferens tied/removed via scrotal incision) vs Tubectomy (part of fallopian tube tied/removed via abdominal/vaginal incision).
  • Amniocentesis – genuine use (detects genetic disorders/chromosomal abnormalities) vs its statutory ban (misuse for sex determination & female foeticide).
  • Two advantages of condoms over other barriers – disposable/self-inserted/give privacy, and uniquely protect against STDs & AIDS.
  • Emergency contraception – progesterone or progesterone-oestrogen or IUD insertion, within 72 hours of coitus, for rape or casual unprotected intercourse.
  • Why some STDs are curable and others are not – all STDs except hepatitis-B, genital herpes and HIV are completely curable if detected and treated early.
Answer-writing tip: this chapter has almost no experiments/diagrams to draw for full marks – the 5-mark answers are scored on complete, correctly-labelled classification. Always write in the sequence: define/classify → name each type with a real example (Lippes loop, CuT, Progestasert; ZIFT/IUT/GIFT) → state its exact mode of action in the textbook's own words.
Never lose these marks: never mix up vasectomy (male, vas deferens) with tubectomy (female, fallopian tube), or ZIFT (embryo up to 8 blastomeres transferred to the fallopian tube) with IUT (embryo with more than 8 blastomeres transferred to the uterus) – these are the most commonly swapped pairs in this chapter.

Fig 3.5 — IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation) & Embryo Transfer (ET) Flowchart 5 M ★★★ Top Priority
