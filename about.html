Narendra Naslai Sir is the Founder & CEO of the BAIO Learning System, an educational platform built to help NEET and 1st/2nd PUC Biology students learn smarter, not harder.

Narendra Sir built this platform around a clear promise: learn better, stay motivated, and achieve stronger results with focused Biology preparation tools in one place.

With hands-on experience in both Biology education and app development, Narendra Naslai designed BAIO Learning System to feel like a smart study partner — one that adapts, rewards consistent effort, and keeps pace with exam trends through continuous content updates.
