1🌳 Do Plants Breathe?
Respiration

Oxidation of C–C bonds in cells · energy released step by step · trapped as ATP (energy currency)

Gas exchange

Stomata + lenticels, by diffusion · no respiratory organs

Why no lungs

Each part serves itself · low demand · every living cell near the surface

2🧪 Glycolysis (EMP)
Where · who

Cytoplasm · all organisms · Embden, Meyerhof, Parnas · 10 steps, no O₂

Key steps

ATP used: glucose → G-6-P (hexokinase), F-6-P → F-1,6-BP · NADH: PGAL → BPGA · ATP made: BPGA → PGA, PEP → pyruvate

Yield

Glucose → 2 pyruvate + 2 ATP (net) + 2 NADH

3🍺 Fermentation
Alcoholic (yeast)

Pyruvate → ethanol + CO₂ · pyruvic acid decarboxylase, alcohol dehydrogenase

Lactic acid (muscle, bacteria)

Pyruvate → lactic acid · lactate dehydrogenase · no CO₂

Limits

NADH → NAD⁺ · < 7% energy · net 2 ATP · yeast dies at 13% alcohol

4🔄 Link Reaction & Krebs
Matrix

Pyruvate + CoA + NAD⁺ → acetyl CoA + CO₂ + NADH (PDH, Mg²⁺)

TCA cycle (Hans Krebs)

Acetyl CoA + OAA → citric acid · per turn 3 NADH, 1 FADH₂, 1 GTP, 2 CO₂

5⚡ ETS & ATP Synthase
Inner membrane

I → UQ (+ II) → III → cyt c → IV → O₂ → H₂O (final acceptor)

ATP

Complex V: F₀ channel + F₁ head · 4H⁺ per ATP · NADH 3, FADH₂ 2 ATP

6🧮 Balance Sheet
Net gain

38 ATP per glucose (theoretical) · glycolysis 8 + link 6 + Krebs 24

Assumptions

Orderly pathway · glycolytic NADH enters mitochondria · no intermediates withdrawn · only glucose respired

vs fermentation

Complete vs partial · 38 vs 2 ATP · NADH fast vs slow

7🔀 Amphibolic Pathway
Entry points

Fatty acids → acetyl CoA · glycerol → PGAL · amino acids (deaminated) → pyruvate / acetyl CoA / Krebs

Both ways

Same intermediates withdrawn for synthesis → catabolism + anabolism = amphibolic

8📊 Respiratory Quotient
RQ

CO₂ evolved / O₂ consumed · depends on the substrate

Values

Carbohydrate 1.0 · protein ~0.9 · fat (tripalmitin 102/145) 0.7

Real life

Substrates are mixed; pure fats or proteins are never used
