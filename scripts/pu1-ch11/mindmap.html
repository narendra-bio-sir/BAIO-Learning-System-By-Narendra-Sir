1🔬 Early Experiments
Who found what

Priestley plants restore air · Ingenhousz sunlight, green parts · Sachs starch, chloroplasts · Engelmann first action spectrum (Cladophora)

van Niel

O₂ comes from H₂O, not CO₂ · 2H₂A + CO₂ → 2A + CH₂O + H₂O

Equation

6CO₂ + 12H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6H₂O + 6O₂

2🌿 Chloroplast & Pigments
Division of labour

Grana (thylakoids) → light reaction: ATP + NADPH · stroma → enzymes fix CO₂ into sugar

4 pigments

Chl a blue-green (chief) · chl b yellow-green · xanthophyll yellow · carotenoid yellow-orange

Spectra

Max absorption in blue & red · accessory pigments widen range, protect chl a

3☀️ Light Reaction
Photosystems

LHC = antenna + reaction centre · PS I = P700 · PS II = P680 (named in order of discovery)

Photolysis

2H₂O → 4H⁺ + O₂ + 4e⁻ · linked to PS II · needs Mn, Cl

Hill reaction (1939)

Isolated chloroplasts + acceptor release O₂ without CO₂

4⚡ Z Scheme & Cyclic
Non-cyclic (Z)

H₂O → P680 → ETS → P700 → NADP reductase → NADPH · gives ATP + NADPH + O₂

Cyclic

PS I only · stroma lamellae · light > 680 nm · ATP only (no NADPH, no O₂)

5🔋 Chemiosmosis
Peter Mitchell (1961)

H⁺ pile up in the lumen (water splitting, H carrier, NADP reductase) → proton gradient

ATP synthase

H⁺ flow through CF₀ → CF₁ changes shape → ATP · ~3 H⁺ per ATP

6🔁 Calvin (C₃) Cycle
Melvin Calvin · ¹⁴C

Acceptor RuBP (5C) · enzyme RuBisCO · first product 3-PGA

3 stages

Carboxylation · reduction · regeneration

Cost

Per CO₂: 3 ATP + 2 NADPH · per glucose: 18 ATP + 12 NADPH, 6 turns

7🌽 C₄ Pathway
Hatch & Slack

Maize, sugarcane, sorghum · Kranz = wreath of bundle sheath cells

Two cells

Mesophyll: PEP + CO₂ → OAA (PEPcase, no RuBisCO) · bundle sheath: CO₂ released → Calvin cycle

8💨 Photorespiration
C₂ pathway (C₃ plants)

RuBisCO binds O₂ → 1 PGA + 1 phosphoglycolate

Wasteful

No sugar, no ATP; ATP used, CO₂ lost · chloroplast, peroxisome, mitochondrion

C₄ plants

High CO₂ at RuBisCO → no photorespiration

9📈 Factors
Blackman (1905)

Rate set by the factor nearest its minimum

Light · CO₂ · temp · water

Light saturates at ~10% sunlight · CO₂ 0.03–0.04% is the major limit · C₄ optimum 30–45 °C · water stress closes stomata
