1🔄 The Cell Cycle
Definition

Duplicate genome → make cell parts → divide into 2 daughter cells · under genetic control

Duration

Human cell ~24 h · yeast ~90 min · M phase only ~1 h

Two phases

Interphase (>95%, preparation) + M phase = karyokinesis (P, M, A, T) + cytokinesis

2⏳ Interphase
G₁ · S · G₂

G₁ growth, no DNA replication · S DNA 2C → 4C, still 2n, centriole duplicates · G₂ proteins for mitosis

G₀ — quiescent

Exit from G₁ · active but not dividing · e.g. heart cells

Where is mitosis?

Animals: diploid somatic cells only · plants: haploid & diploid

3🧬 Prophase & Metaphase
Mitosis = equational

Strasburger (1870, plants) · Flemming (1879, animals; coined "mitosis")

Prophase

Chromatin condenses · centrosomes to poles · asters + spindle = mitotic apparatus

Metaphase

Kinetochores hold fibres · metaphase plate · best stage to count chromosomes

4↔️ Anaphase & Telophase
Anaphase

Centromeres split · chromatids to opposite poles · centromere leads, arms trail

Telophase

Chromosomes decondense · envelope, nucleolus, Golgi, ER reform → 2 daughter nuclei (reverse of prophase)

5✂️ Cytokinesis & Significance
Animal vs plant

Furrow (animal) · cell plate = middle lamella (plant)

Syncytium

No cytokinesis → multinucleate · coconut liquid endosperm

Why mitosis?

Growth · repair · N/C ratio · meristems

6🔀 Meiosis & Prophase I
Meiosis = reductional

2 divisions, 1 DNA replication → 4 haploid cells · gametogenesis

L · Z · P · D · D

Leptotene visible · Zygotene synapsis + synaptonemal complex · Pachytene crossing over (recombinase) · Diplotene chiasmata · Diakinesis terminalisation

7➗ Meiosis I
Metaphase I

Bivalents on the equatorial plate

Anaphase I

Homologues separate; sister chromatids stay joined (2n → n)

Telophase I · interkinesis

Envelope reappears → dyad · interkinesis is short with no DNA replication

8🌱 Meiosis II & Significance
Like mitosis (equational)

Metaphase II: fibres to kinetochores of sister chromatids · Anaphase II: centromeres split

Telophase II

Tetrad = 4 haploid cells

Significance

Keeps chromosome number constant · crossing over → variation → raw material for evolution
