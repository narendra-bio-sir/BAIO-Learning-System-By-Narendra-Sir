BAIO Learning System · Install Guide

Install the BAIO Learning App in under a minute

BAIO isn't downloaded from the Play Store or App Store — it installs straight from your browser as an app icon on your phone or laptop. No app store, no separate download. Pick your device below and follow the steps.

📲 Works on Android, iPhone, and any laptop
STEP 1

Install on Android (Chrome)

1
Open Chrome and go to the BAIO website.
2
Tap the ⋮ (three-dot) menu in the top-right corner.
3
Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen) and confirm.
⋮ Menu → Install app → Install
4
The BAIO icon now appears on your home screen. Tap it to open the app — full screen, no browser bar, just like any other app.
STEP 2

Install on iPhone / iPad (Safari)

On iPhone this must be done in Safari — Chrome on iPhone can't install apps to the home screen.

1
Open Safari and go to the BAIO website.
2
Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen → Add
4
Tap Add in the top-right corner. The BAIO icon now sits on your home screen like any other app.
STEP 3

Install on a Laptop or Desktop

Works in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, or Chromebook.

1
Open the BAIO website in Chrome or Edge.
2
Look for the install icon (a small ⊕ / monitor icon) at the right end of the address bar, and click it.
3
Click Install in the popup that appears.
Address bar ⊕ icon → Install
4
BAIO now opens as its own app window from your desktop, taskbar, or Start Menu — no need to open a browser tab first.

Don't see the install icon? Not a problem — you can always just open the website link in your browser and use it normally; nothing is locked behind installing.

STEP 4

Fastest Way: Scan the QR Code

Open your phone's camera app and point it at this QR code. Tap the link that pops up — it opens the BAIO website directly, and from there just follow the Android or iPhone steps above to add it to your home screen.

STEP 5

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay or search the Play Store / App Store?

No. BAIO is a web app — there's nothing to search for or buy in an app store. Just visit the website link and install it from your browser as shown above.

How much space does it take on my phone?

Only a few MB — far less than a typical app store app, since it loads content as you use it.

Will it work without internet?

Once installed, the app shell loads instantly and shows an offline banner if your connection drops, but you'll need internet to load new chapters, quizzes, and updates.

I don't see "Install app" in my Chrome menu — what now?

Make sure Chrome is updated to the latest version. If it's still missing, use Add to Home screen instead — it gives the same full-screen app icon.

I installed it — now what?

Open the BAIO icon from your home screen, then head to the Student Guide to learn how sections, chapters, and codes work.
